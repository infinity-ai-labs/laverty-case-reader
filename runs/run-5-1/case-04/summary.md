# Mazen Riad Al Attar v Qaws Al Nada General Trading LLC

Labour claim by Mazen Riad Al Attar (employed since 01/03/2022 on AED 22,000, AED 14,000 basic) against Qaws Al Nada LLC for AED 121,300 for July-August 2026 wages, 24 days leave pay, end-of-service gratuity and notice pay after termination on 31/08/2026 without notice. Amicable settlement of complaint AUH-2026-47102 failed despite employer's offer of AED 60,000 in instalments, so MOHRE referred the case to Abu Dhabi Labour Court on 28/09/2026 under Article 54, filed 08/10/2026. A handwritten note records a later verbal AED 85,000 offer rejected and first hearing on 10/11/2026.

- Employer admitted wage delay for financial reasons, disputed notice claiming mutual termination, offered in writing on 15/09/2026 AED 60,000 in three monthly instalments as final settlement. (page 2)
- Worker rejected the offer at settlement session on Wednesday 16/09/2026 and insisted on full AED 121,300 claim. (page 2)
- Ministry views worker entitled to wages, leave pay and gratuity, leaves notice pay to court, and refers case as claim exceeds AED 50,000. (page 2)
- Claim seeks order for defendant to pay claimant AED 121,300 plus fees, costs and lawyer fees. (page 1)
