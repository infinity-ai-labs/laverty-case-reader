## Key facts

- On 16/06/2026 around 21:40 at Umm Suqeim-Al Wasl signalised intersection in Dubai, first defendant ran the red light and hit the left side of claimant's vehicle which was crossing on green. (page 1)
- Traffic report put 100% liability on first-vehicle driver and none on claimant, based on camera recording and eyewitness. (page 2)
- Claimant suffered comminuted tibia-fibula fracture of right leg and right ankle fracture with bruises, hospitalised 16/06-29/06/2026, operated 17/06/2026 then 36 physiotherapy sessions 06/07-30/09/2026. (page 3)
- Given sick leave 16/06-15/10/2026 and fit to return from 16/10/2026; monthly salary 19,500 AED used for lost-earnings calculation. (page 3)
- Left with 15% partial permanent disability of right lower limb with stiffness, limited movement and slight limp. (page 3)
- First defendant convicted by Traffic Misdemeanor Court on 28/07/2026 in case 3471/2026 and fined AED 10,000; judgment final per 03/09/2026 certificate. (page 1)
- First defendant's vehicle insured with second defendant under policy No. 000000. (page 1)
