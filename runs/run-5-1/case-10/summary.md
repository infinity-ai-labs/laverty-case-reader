# Ratification of Arbitration Award No. 47 of 2026 - Naseem Al Jabal v Al Qaws Al Aali

Naseem Al Jabal applied on 20/10/2026 to ratify the unanimous award dated 10/09/2026 in Case 47/2026 of White Sail Centre, ordering Al Qaws Al Aali to pay AED 846,300 supply value plus AED 96,400 arbitration costs plus AED 40,000 fees totalling AED 982,700 plus 5% yearly interest from arbitration request on 06/04/2026. Award was announced 15/09/2026 and 30-day nullification time expired 15/10/2026 with no action. On 26/10/2026 the Court of Appeal ratified the award with executory formula and costs, subject to 30-day challenge under Article 57. A commercial execution file was registered on 28/10/2026.
