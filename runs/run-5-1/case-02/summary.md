# Blue Horizon Building Materials Supply LLC v. Golden Falcon Contracting Establishment (owner Rashed Hamad Al Falasi)

Blue Horizon Building Materials Supply LLC claims AED 386,500 balance from Golden Falcon Contracting Establishment for rebar and cement supplied under 10/01/2026 agreement under invoices totalling AED 506,500, of which only AED 120,000 was paid by transfer; a AED 150,000 cheque bounced for insufficient funds. A notarial demand dated 01/09/2026 was served 03/09/2026 with 15 days to pay, with 6% late interest per Clause 9, with no payment. A commercial claim dated 05/10/2026 (filed 06/10/2026) before Dubai Court of First Instance seeks principal, 6% interest from each invoice due date, and fees, with hearing fixed 24/11/2026. The 30/09/2026 account statement supports the balance after handwritten correction from 380,500 to 386,500.
