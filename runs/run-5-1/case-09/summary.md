# Maryam Al Zaabi v Marfa Al Nujoom Motors – vehicle replacement, refund and compensation

Claimant who bought a new car with warranty seeks primary replacement or alternative refund of AED 136,500 for a recurring manufacturing gearbox defect (three times) plus AED 32,920 for rental and damages. Job card confirms warranty repairs and manufacturing-defect finding with repeat repair.

## Key facts

- Claimant bought 2026 Alkuria Sarab 2.0 Turbo from defendant on 16/06/2026 for AED 136,500 with 3-year / 100,000 km warranty and received it same day (page 1)
- Gearbox fault appeared (judder and delayed shifting); car entered defendant's workshop under job card No. JC-2026-04417 (page 1)
- Job card details: received 28/07/2026, mileage 3,184 km, warranty valid, complaint judder on take-off and delayed 2nd-to-3rd shift with gearbox fault warning, delivered 08/08/2026 at no charge under warranty (page 2)
- Technician confirmed manufacturing defect: slipping in third-gear clutch and high gearbox oil temperature (page 2, handwriting)
- Same fault recurred and was repaired a second time on same card on 24/08/2026 with control unit replacement, then failed a third time on 06/10/2026 (page 1)
- Defendant on 13/10/2026 refused claimant's 08/10/2026 request for replacement or refund within seven days (page 1)

# مريم الزعابي ضد شركة مرفأ النجوم للسيارات – مطالبة استبدال سيارة ورد ثمنها وتعويض

تطالب المدعية التي اشترت سيارة جديدة بضمان باستبدالها أصلياً أو فسخ البيع ورد ثمنها 136,500 درهم احتياطياً لتكرار عيب مصنعي في ناقل الحركة ثلاث مرات مع تعويض 32,920 درهماً اجمالياً. تؤكد بطاقة العمل الاصلاح تحت الضمان وإثبات العيب المصنعي وتكراره.
