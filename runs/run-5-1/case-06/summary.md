# Naseem Al Ghaf Foodstuff Trading LLC v Mubarak Salem Bin Ateeq Al Qamzi - Objection to rent increase on renewal and to eviction demand

Tenant company of a Ras Al Khor warehouse (2026 contract at AED 210,000) says landlord's 24/09/2026 notice sought to raise rent to AED 252,000 and threatened eviction on 31/12/2026, that receipt was late, and it replied on 15/10/2026 rejecting the increase. It seeks renewal at same rent, alternatively fair rent capped at AED 220,500, and rejection of eviction, relying on Articles 14, 6, 13, 9 and 25(2) of Law 26/2007 and Article 1 of Decree 43/2013.

## Key facts

- Leased premises: warehouse No. (9), Ras Al Khor Industrial Second, Dubai, under Ejari contract No. 000000, from 01/01/2026 to 31/12/2026, at annual rent AED 210,000. (page 1)
- Landlord sent notice dated 24/09/2026 to increase annual rent on renewal to AED 252,000 (20%), giving 15 days from its date to agree, otherwise contract deemed ended on 31/12/2026 and eviction required. (page 1)
- Claimant states notice was hand-delivered and only received on the handwritten receipt date, after the period had passed, and replied on 15/10/2026 rejecting the increase and offering renewal at same rent. (page 1)
- Claimant argues comparable average rent per rental index is AED 240,000, so maximum lawful new rent does not exceed AED 220,500. (page 1)
- Handwritten note at bottom of notice records receipt of original on 16/10/2026 with company stamp and signature. (page 2, handwriting)
