## Key facts

- Company is Nakhlat Al Sabah Foodstuff Trading LLC, Dubai trade licence 000000, capital AED 300,000 (300 shares) (page 2)
- Karim Al Sharif holds 135 shares (45%) and is manager per MOA; Faisal Al Hammadi 120 shares (40%); Salma Al Naqbi 45 shares (15%) (page 2)
- Ordinary general meeting held 30/09/2026 with 100% quorum, chaired by manager with Nader Al Ali as secretary, on invitation dated 07/09/2026 (page 2)
- Meeting approved by 60% the 2025 balance sheet and profit/loss, discharge of manager and new auditor appointment, with Faisal Al Hammadi (40%) objecting (page 2)
- Faisal Al Hammadi signed with objection to resolutions 2, 3 and 4: budget unaudited and manager may not vote on his discharge (page 2, handwriting)
- Auditor abstained from approving 2025 financials for lack of supporting documents for AED 1,284,600; his letter reached the company about two weeks before the meeting per incoming stamp (page 1, stamp)
- Unsupported items are AED 418,500 cash withdrawals, AED 596,000 payments to Eastern company without contract, AED 270,100 inventory shortage, total 1,284,600 (page 3)
- Manager did not answer claimant request dated 05/10/2026 to inspect company books within seven days (page 1)
