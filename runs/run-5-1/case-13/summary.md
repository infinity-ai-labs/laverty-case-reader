## Key facts

- Claimant undertook to build a villa for defendant in Al Awir Al Oula for 1,480,000 AED under lump-sum contract dated 12/01/2026 within 8 months from site handover on 02/02/2026 under Al Mirsam supervision (page 1)
- Variation order No. 1 for 46,500 AED was approved on 14/04/2026 bringing total completed works to 1,526,500 AED (page 3)
- Consultant carried out joint inspection on 17/09/2026 and issued completion certificate on 21/09/2026 showing handwritten actual completion date 14/09/2026 with minor snags to be done within 14 days (page 2, handwriting)
- Works became fit for intended use except minor snags not preventing handover; 12-month maintenance period runs from actual completion date (page 2)
- Contractor submitted bank maintenance guarantee on 22/09/2026 and final payment certificate No. 8 issued on 24/09/2026 for net due 167,850 AED after deducting retention and prior payments of 1,206,000 AED (page 1)
- Defendant took over villa on 05/10/2026 after snags completion and refused payment despite formal notice on 12/10/2026 (page 1)
- Consultant hand-corrected retention amount from 125,650 to 152,650 AED equal to 10% of total works (page 3, handwriting)
