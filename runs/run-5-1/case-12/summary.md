# Zahranova Perfumes v Ghaimat Al Raihan Perfume Trading – Registered trademark infringement claim

Zahranova company claims it owns registered mark ZAHRANOVA No. 000417 class 3 and that the defendant sold bottles bearing the mark at a low price proved by a 24/09/2026 test purchase, then obtained a conservatory order executed by seizure on 08/10/2026 valued at AED 201,250, and seeks confirmation of seizure, injunction, confiscation and destruction plus AED 350,000 compensation under Articles 17, 18, 21, 47, 47-bis, 48 and 50 of Federal Decree-Law No. 36 of 2021.

- Ex parte order No. 731 of 2026 for description and conservatory seizure issued 06/10/2026 and executed by seizure on 08/10/2026 (page 1)
