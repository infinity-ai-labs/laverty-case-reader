- Cheque 000731 dated 15/06/2026 for AED 96,400 — return reason: insufficient funds. (page 1)
- Cheque 000732 dated 15/07/2026 for AED 112,750 — return reason: insufficient funds. (page 1)
- Cheque 000733 dated 15/08/2026 for AED 88,600 — return reason: signature mismatch. (page 1)
- Al Kuthban National Bank certified presentment on the drawer's account on 15/06, 15/07 and 17/08 and return on 16/06, 16/07 and 18/08, and reported the two insufficient-funds cheques to the Central Bank. (page 2)
- On 09/09/2026 the urgent judge in Request No. 412/2026 ordered precautionary attachment of the respondent's funds with banks operating in the State up to AED 297,750. (page 3, handwriting)
