## Key facts

- Claimant joined defendant on 01/03/2019 under indefinite contract as Senior Accountant on AED 18,000 total monthly, AED 12,000 basic (page 1)
- Defendant stopped paying wages from June 2026; claimant complained to MOHRE on 04/08/2026 No. MO-2026-58213 (page 1)
- On 15/08/2026, 11 days after complaint, defendant terminated claimant effective same date without prior notice or payment (page 1)
- Amicable settlement at Ministry failed so dispute referred to court on 22/09/2026 as claim exceeds AED 50,000 (page 1)
- Claimant counsel and defendant counsel attended 27/10/2026 hearing before Judge Khaled Salem Al Naeimi; defendant counsel filed POA and sought adjournment to review and reply (page 3)
- Court adjourned to 17/11/2026, ordering defendant to file reply with documents by 10/11/2026 and to submit original contract and Jan-Aug 2026 payrolls (page 3)

# سامر خليل الحداد ضد شركة نجمة الخليج للمقاولات العامة ذ.م.م – مطالبة عمالية بالأجور ومكافأة نهاية الخدمة والتعويض عن الإنهاء غير المشروع
