# Naseem Al Ghaf Foodstuff Trading LLC v Mubarak Salem Bin Ateeq Al Qamzi - Objection to Rent Increase and Eviction Demand

Tenant company filed a Dubai tenancy claim challenging the landlord's 24/09/2026 notice raising annual rent from AED 210,000 to AED 252,000 (20%) and demanding vacating on 31/12/2026. Tenant says the hand-delivered notice was only received on 12/10/2026, after the 90-day period, and it replied on 15/10/2026 rejecting the increase. It seeks to disregard the notice, renew at the same rent, alternatively cap renewal rent at AED 220,500, and reject eviction.

## Key facts

- Leased premises is warehouse No. (9), Ras Al Khor Industrial Area Second, Dubai, under Ejari-registered contract No. 000000 from 01/01/2026 to 31/12/2026 at annual rent AED 210,000. (page 1)
- Landlord sent notice dated 24/09/2026 increasing renewal annual rent to AED 252,000 (20%), giving 15 days to accept, otherwise contract deemed ended on 31/12/2026 and tenant must vacate. (page 1)
- Notice was delivered by hand and claimant only received it on the handwritten receipt date of 12/10/2026, after the 90-day period had passed. (page 2, handwriting)
- Claimant replied on 15/10/2026 rejecting the increase and offering renewal at the same rent. (page 1)
- Landlord asked for written confirmation and signing of renewal within 15 days of notice, otherwise contract deemed expired and eviction with legal procedures. (page 2)
- Tenant filed the claim form dated 20/10/2026 under ref NG/ADM/2026/31 before the Dubai Rental Disputes Center. (page 1)
