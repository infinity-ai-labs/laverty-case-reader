# Wamdat Al Noor v Aamidet Al Shurooq — bounced cheques claim (AED 297,750)

Wamdat Al Noor alleges Aamidet Al Shurooq issued it three cheques under a supply agreement dated 10/02/2026 drawn on Al Kuthban National Bank totalling AED 297,750 which were dishonoured (two for insufficient funds, one for signature mismatch) per the bank certificate dated 03/09/2026. On 09/09/2026 in Request No. 412/2026 the Urgent Matters Judge ordered precautionary attachment of the debtor's bank balances up to that amount and ordered the creditor to file a substantive claim within 8 days. The creditor filed this commercial action dated 14/09/2026 (filed 15/09/2026) before Sharjah Federal Court of First Instance seeking validation of the attachment, payment with 5% interest, and fees and attorney's fees.
