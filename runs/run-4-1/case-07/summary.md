# Mazen Ibrahim Al Attar v. Hisham Tawfiq Al Badri and Sanam Al Rimal Insurance P.S.C. - Compensation for traffic accident

Civil compensation claim before Dubai First Instance Court where injured driver Mazen Al Attar sues the at-fault driver who ran a red light on 16/06/2026 and his insurer jointly for AED 346,640 (treatment 58,640, lost wages 78,000, 15% disability 150,000, moral damage 60,000). Traffic report holds first driver 100% liable, criminal court fined him AED 10,000 finally, and medical report proves fractures, surgery and 15% permanent disability.

## Key facts

- First defendant ran the red light at Umm Suqeim - Al Wasl intersection in Dubai and hit the left side of claimant's vehicle which was crossing on green, as confirmed by camera recording and eyewitness. (page 1)
- Traffic report 0000/2026 assigned 100% responsibility to first defendant and recorded claimant as not at fault. (page 2)
- Claimant suffered comminuted tibia-fibula fracture in right leg and right ankle fracture with chest and left arm contusions, underwent open reduction internal fixation then 36 physiotherapy sessions. (page 3)
- Claimant was hospitalised from 16/06/2026 to 29/06/2026 and was on sick leave until 15/10/2026. (page 3)
- Claimant was left with 15% partial permanent disability in right lower limb with partial ankle stiffness and slight limp. (page 1)
- 15% (fifteen percent) permanent partial disability rate is handwritten in the medical report. (page 3, handwriting)
- Traffic misdemeanor court convicted first defendant on 28/07/2026 in case 3471/2026 and fined him AED 10,000; judgment became final for lack of appeal per 03/09/2026 certificate. (page 1)
- At-fault vehicle is insured with second defendant Sanam Al Rimal Insurance P.S.C. under policy 000000. (page 1)
- Claimant's monthly salary is AED 19,500 and work absence is four months from 16/06/2026 to 15/10/2026. (page 1)
