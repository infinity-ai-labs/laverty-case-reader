- Actual completion date is 14/09/2026 (handwritten); 12-month maintenance period runs from that date (page 2, handwriting)
- Handwritten snag list: minor water leak under guest WC sink, repaint parts of outer fence, install kitchen cabinet handles — to be done within 14 days (page 2, handwriting)
- Consultant issued completion certificate on 21/09/2026, bank maintenance guarantee provided 22/09/2026, Final Payment Certificate No. 8 issued 24/09/2026 (page 1)

- Defendant received villa on 05/10/2026 after snags and refused payment despite formal notice on 12/10/2026 (page 1)
