# Blue Horizon Building Materials Supply LLC v Golden Falcon Contracting Establishment and its owner Rashid Hamad Al Falasi

Blue Horizon claims AED 386,500 balance from three building-material invoices totalling AED 506,500 less a AED 120,000 bank payment, after a AED 150,000 cheque bounced for insufficient funds, plus 6% late interest and costs. Notarial notice dated 01/09/2026 was served 03/09/2026, claim dated 05/10/2026 (filed 06/10/2026), hearing set 24/11/2026.

## Key facts

- Claimant supplied building materials (rebar and cement) to Defendant's Al Khawaneej project under a 10/01/2026 supply agreement, received without reservation under three invoices totalling AED 506,500. (page 1)
- Defendant paid AED 120,000 by bank transfer on 10/05/2026. (page 1)
- Cheque No. 004512 for AED 150,000 dated 30/06/2026 was returned unpaid on 02/07/2026 for insufficient funds and does not count as payment. (page 1)
- Remaining balance due is AED 386,500 after deducting the bank payment from the total. (page 3, handwriting)
- Notice was sent via notary on 01/09/2026 and served on 03/09/2026 demanding payment within 15 days of receipt, which was not paid. (page 2)
- Parties agreed in clause 9 of the agreement to 6% annual late interest; Claimant does not claim compound interest. (page 2)
