# Al Bareeq Al Faddi Kitchen Equipment Trading v Layali Al Marsa Restaurant Management - Payment Order Application

Application dated 12/10/2026 to the Payment Order Judge at Sharjah Federal Court of First Instance by Al Bareeq Al Faddi against Layali Al Marsa seeking an order for AED 293,450 net value of commercial kitchen equipment under three invoices less a credit note, plus 5% yearly interest from application date and fees/expenses. Relies on written debt acknowledgment dated 06/08/2026 due 31/08/2026, and a notarial demand dated 01/10/2026 with 7 days to pay that expired unpaid, citing Articles 143 and 144 of Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022. Acknowledgment printed amount 318,450 was hand-corrected to 293,450.

## Key facts

- Claimant supplied commercial kitchen equipment to respondent's branches in Sharjah, received without reservation under invoices SG-2026-041 dated 09/03/2026 for AED 126,000, SG-2026-077 dated 21/04/2026 for AED 104,250, and SG-2026-112 dated 02/06/2026 for AED 88,200, less credit note CN-2026-019 dated 18/06/2026 for AED (25,000) (returned goods), leaving net due AED 293,450. (page 1)
- Respondent acknowledged the debt in writing dated 06/08/2026 signed by its General Manager Mr. Wael Jamal Al-Khatib, confirming full receipt of equipment in sound condition without reservation, undertaking to pay on the stated date, and admitting it is an established, due and undisputed debt. (page 2)
- Debt reason is value of commercial kitchen equipment supplied to our branches in Sharjah under invoices Nos. SG-2026-041, SG-2026-077 and SG-2026-112, payable in one payment due no later than 31/08/2026. (page 2)
- Claimant demanded payment by notice served via the Notary Public dated 01/10/2026 granting a 7-day period from notification to pay; the period expired without payment. (page 1)
- Printed debt amount in acknowledgment (318,450) was hand-corrected to 293,450 - W.Gh. (page 2, handwriting)
