# Mariam Khaled Al Zaabi v Marfa Al Nujoom Motors LLC – vehicle replacement or refund and compensation

Mariam Al Zaabi says she bought a new car with warranty from Marfa Al Nujoom Motors, a manufacturing gearbox defect repeated three times despite two repairs, and the dealer refused replacement or refund. She seeks primary replacement, alternatively termination and refund of AED 136,500, and in either case rental costs AED 7,920 plus AED 25,000 damages totalling AED 32,920 with fees, relying on consumer protection and civil transactions laws, with a job card confirming a factory defect.

## Key facts

- Claimant bought from defendant on 16/06/2026 an Alcoria Sarab 2.0 Turbo model 2026 for AED 136,500 with 3-year or 100,000 km warranty and received it same day. (page 1)
- Gearbox fault appeared (judder and delayed shifting) and car was taken to defendant's workshop under job card No. JC-2026-04417. (page 1)
- Job card receipt 28/07/2026 09:40 for Alcoria Sarab 2.0 Turbo 2026, mileage 3,184 km, warranty valid, technician Wael Fahmy Abdelqader, complaint of judder on launch, delayed 2nd to 3rd shift and gearbox fault warning. (page 2)
- Work done under warranty: gearbox diagnosis (D-100), control unit software update (S-210), valve body replacement and oil change (T-355), nothing due from customer, delivery date 08/08/2026. (page 2)
- Technician noted by hand that inspection showed third-gear clutch slip and high gearbox oil temperature, that it is a manufacturing defect, and repair was done. (page 2, handwriting)
- Same fault recurred and was repaired again under same card, then car returned with same fault and was re-repaired on 24/8/2026 with control unit replacement. (page 2, handwriting)
- Car failed a third time on 06/10/2026, claimant requested on 08/10/2026 replacement or refund within seven days and defendant refused on 13/10/2026. (page 1)
