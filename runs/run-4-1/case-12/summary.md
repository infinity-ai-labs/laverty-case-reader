# Zahranova v. Gheimat Al Rihan – Registered trademark infringement claim

Zahranova, owner of mark ZAHRANOVA No. 000417 Class 3, sues Gheimat Al Rihan perfume trading in Dubai Court of First Instance for infringement. Relies on a 24/09/2026 test purchase and a precautionary description/seizure order No. 731 of 2026 issued 06/10/2026 and executed 08/10/2026, seizing goods valued at AED 201,250. Asks to confirm seizure, enjoin use, confiscate and destroy goods at defendant's cost, and award AED 350,000 compensation plus fees, reserving criminal rights. Hearing handwritten as 09/11/2026; filing stamp 22/10/2026.
