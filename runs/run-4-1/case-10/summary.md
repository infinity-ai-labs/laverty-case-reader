# Request for Ratification and Enforcement of Arbitration Award No. 47 of 2026 - Nassim Al Jabal v Al Qaws Al Aali

Nassim Al Jabal seeks ratification of the unanimous final award dated 10/09/2026 in arbitration 47/2026 at White Sail Centre ordering Al Qaws Al Aali to pay AED 846,300 for supplies plus AED 96,400 arbitration costs plus AED 40,000 counsel fees plus 5% yearly interest, with no nullity action filed in time. On 26/10/2026 in Request 318/2026 the Dubai Court of Appeal ratified the award with executory formula plus costs, and commercial enforcement 752/2026 was registered on 28/10/2026.

## Key facts

- Under supply contract dated 12/01/2026 claimant supplies ready-mix and precast elements to respondent's project in Dubai; clause 18 provides for arbitration at White Sail Commercial Arbitration Centre (page 1)
- Respondent failed to pay balance of supplies so claimant filed arbitration on 06/04/2026 (page 1)
- Tribunal issued unanimous final award on 10/09/2026 in case 47/2026 ordering respondent to pay 846,300 supply value plus 96,400 arbitration costs plus 40,000 counsel fees totalling 982,700 plus 5% annual interest on first amount from arbitration request until payment, rejecting counterclaim for delay penalties (page 2)
- Award was announced to respondent on 15/09/2026 and 30-day nullity period expired on 15/10/2026 with no action filed (page 1)
- Applicant attached award original, arbitration agreement copy and deposit minutes required by Article 55(1); no nullity ground proved, dispute arbitrable, no public order violation (page 3)
- Court ordered on 26/10/2026 ratification of award with executory formula for full enforcement and respondent to bear fees and costs; grievance allowed within 30 days from day after announcement under Article 57 (page 3)
- Commercial enforcement file No. 752/2026 was registered on 28/10/2026 per handwritten note (page 3, handwriting)
