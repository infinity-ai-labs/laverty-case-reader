## Key facts

- On 16/06/2026 around 21:40 at Umm Suqeim-Al Wasl intersection Dubai (signal-controlled), first defendant ran red light and hit left side of claimant vehicle which was crossing on green. (page 1)
- Traffic report apportions liability: 100% at fault first vehicle driver Hisham Tawfiq Al Badri, not at fault Mazen Ibrahim Al Attar. (page 2)
- Claimant suffered comminuted tibia-fibula fracture right leg and right ankle fracture with contusions, had open reduction internal fixation on 17/06/2026, stayed hospital until 29/06/2026. (page 3)
- Claimant had 36 physiotherapy sessions 06/07/2026-30/09/2026, sick leave 16/06/2026-15/10/2026, fit to return to work from 16/10/2026. (page 3)
- Claimant left with 15% partial permanent disability right lower limb with partial ankle stiffness and slight limp. (page 3)
