# Silver Bright Kitchen Equipment Trading LLC v Layali Al Marsa Restaurant Management LLC - Payment Order Petition

Payment order petition dated 12/10/2026 by Silver Bright Kitchen Equipment Trading LLC against Layali Al Marsa Restaurant Management LLC for AED 293,450 net value of kitchen equipment under three invoices less a credit note, relying on a written acknowledgement dated 06/08/2026 signed by GM Wael Jamal Al-Khatib (printed amount 318,450 hand-corrected to 293,450) promising single payment by 31/08/2026, and a notarial demand dated 01/10/2026 giving 7 days which expired unpaid, seeking payment with 5% annual interest from petition date plus fees under Articles 143 and 144 of Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022.

## Key facts

- Claimant supplied commercial kitchen equipment to respondent's branches in Sharjah, received without reservation under three invoices (page 1)
- Invoices: SG-2026-041 dated 09/03/2026 AED 126,000, SG-2026-077 dated 21/04/2026 AED 104,250, SG-2026-112 dated 02/06/2026 AED 88,200, less credit note CN-2026-019 dated 18/06/2026 AED 25,000 for returned goods, net AED 293,450 (page 1)
- Respondent acknowledged the debt in writing by declaration dated 06/08/2026 signed by its General Manager Mr. Wael Jamal Al-Khatib that its liability is engaged to the creditor and it received the equipment complete and in sound condition (page 2)
- Printed debt amount in acknowledgement (318,450) was struck through and hand-corrected to 293,450 (page 2, handwriting)
- Debtor undertook to pay the amount on the stated date in one payment by 31/08/2026 at the latest and acknowledged it as an established, due and undisputed debt (page 2)
- Claimant called on respondent to pay by notice served via Notary Public dated 01/10/2026 granting seven days from notification, which expired without payment (page 1)
