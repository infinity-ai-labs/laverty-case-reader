# Application No. 318/2026 for Ratification and Enforcement of Arbitration Award No. 47/2026 – Naseem Al Jabal Ready-Mix Concrete LLC v Al Qaws Al Aali Construction Contracting LLC

Naseem Al Jabal seeks ratification of the unanimous 10/09/2026 award in Case 47/2026 at White Sail Centre against Al Qaws Al Aali, ordering payment of AED 846,300 supply value plus AED 96,400 costs plus AED 40,000 fees totalling AED 982,700 with 5% yearly interest from 06/04/2026, on grounds Article 55 documents are complete and no nullity suit was filed within 30 days of 15/09/2026 notification. On 26/10/2026 the Dubai Court of Appeal in Application 318/2026 ordered ratification with executory formula and costs on respondent, subject to challenge within 30 days from day after announcement under Article 57; commercial enforcement file 752/2026 was registered on 28/10/2026.
