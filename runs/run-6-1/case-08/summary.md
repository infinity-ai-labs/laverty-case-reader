# Faisal Nasser Al Hammadi v Karim Anwar Al Sharif and Nakhlat Al Sabah Foodstuff Trading LLC — removal of manager, annulment of discharge, audit and claim for AED 1,284,600

A 40% partner sues the 45% partner-manager and their LLC before Dubai Full Commercial Court: auditor refused to sign 2025 statements over AED 1,284,600 unsupported items, manager put unaudited accounts to 30/09/2026 assembly and voted on his own discharge so it passed 60% over claimant's recorded handwritten objection, and ignored a 05/10/2026 books-inspection request. Seeks removal, annulment of discharge, accounting expert for 2025-2026, and preliminary AED 1,284,600 payment to company.

- Manager did not answer claimant's 05/10/2026 request to inspect books within seven days. (page 1)
