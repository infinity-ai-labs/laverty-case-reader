# Samer Khalil Al Haddad v Najma Al Khaleej General Contracting LLC

Partial labour case 1234/2026 before Dubai Labour Court: senior accountant (since 01/03/2019 on AED 18,000) claims AED 196,460 for delayed wages, notice, gratuity, leave and unlawful-termination compensation after dismissal 11 days after MOHRE complaint. At 27/10/2026 hearing both lawyers appeared; court adjourned to 17/11/2026 for defence reply due by 10/11/2026 with original contract and Jan-Aug 2026 payrolls.

- Claimant filed MOHRE complaint on 04/08/2026 registered No. MO-2026-58213 (page 1)
- Defendant terminated claimant in writing effective 15/08/2026, 11 days after complaint, without prior notice or payment (page 1)
- Amicable settlement failed and dispute referred to court on 22/09/2026 as claim exceeds AED 50,000 (page 1)
