# Mariam Al Zaabi v Marfa Al Nujoom Motors - defective car replacement and compensation claim

Mariam Al Zaabi claims she bought a new warranted car from Marfa Al Nujoom Motors on 16/06/2026 with a manufacturing transmission defect that recurred three times despite two repairs, and the dealer refused replacement or refund. She filed on 21/10/2026 before the Sharjah minor commercial court seeking primarily replacement, alternatively termination and AED 136,500 refund, plus in either case AED 7,920 alternative rental and AED 25,000 damages totalling AED 32,920. Job card JC-2026-04417 supports two warranty repairs with handwritten confirmation of a manufacturing defect.

## Key facts

- Claimant bought from defendant on 16/06/2026 an Alcoria Sarab 2.0 Turbo 2026 for AED 136,500 with 3-year or 100,000 km warranty and received it same day (page 1)
- Transmission defect appeared (jolt and delayed shifting); car entered defendant's workshop under job card JC-2026-04417 and was technically confirmed as manufacturing defect (page 1)
- Job card received on 28/07/2026 with odometer 3,184 km; complaint was jerk on take-off, delayed 2nd to 3rd shift and transmission fault warning (page 2)
- Work done under warranty: transmission check (D-100), control unit software update (S-210), valve body replacement and oil change (T-355) at no charge, delivered 08/08/2026 (page 2)
- Handwritten inspection result: slipping of third-gear clutch and high gearbox oil temperature - manufacturing defect - repaired (page 2, handwriting)
- Same defect recurred and was repaired a second time on same card, then failed a third time on 06/10/2026; handwritten re-repair note records return with same defect - re-repaired on 24/8/2026 - control unit replaced (page 2, handwriting)
- Claimant demanded on 08/10/2026 replacement or refund within seven days and defendant refused on 13/10/2026 (page 1)
