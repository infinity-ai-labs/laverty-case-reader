# Mazen Riad Al Attar v Qous Al Nada General Trading LLC - Labour claim for delayed wages and end-of-service dues

Labour claim by Mazen Riad Al Attar against Qous Al Nada General Trading LLC for AED 121,300 (July-Aug 2026 wages, 24 days leave, gratuity, notice pay) after termination on 31/08/2026 without notice. MOHRE complaint AUH-2026-47102 failed to settle and was referred to Abu Dhabi Labour Court on 28/09/2026, filed 08/10/2026. MOHRE supports wages, leave and gratuity, leaves notice to court. Handwritten note records refused AED 85,000 post-referral offer and first hearing 10/11/2026.

## Key facts

- Claimant worked for company since 01/03/2022 on AED 22,000 monthly, AED 14,000 basic, 30 days notice. (page 1)
- July and August 2026 wages unpaid, service ended 31/08/2026 without notice, 24 days leave balance. (page 1)
- Amicable settlement of complaint AUH-2026-47102 of 07/09/2026 failed, MOHRE referred dispute to court on 28/09/2026. (page 2)
- Employer admitted delay for financial reasons, disputed notice pay, offered AED 60,000 in three instalments on 15/09/2026. (page 2)
- Worker refused offer at 16/09/2026 settlement session and insisted on full AED 121,300. (page 2)
- MOHRE found wages, leave and gratuity due, left notice pay to court, referred case as claim exceeds AED 50,000. (page 2)
- Handwritten note records post-referral verbal offer of AED 85,000 refused by client and first hearing on 10/11/2026. (page 2, handwriting)
