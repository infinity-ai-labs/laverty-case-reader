# Blue Horizon Building Materials Supply LLC v Golden Falcon Contracting Establishment - AED 386,500 claim

Blue Horizon Building Materials Supply LLC claims AED 386,500 from Golden Falcon Contracting and its owner as balance for building materials totalling AED 506,500, of which AED 120,000 was paid and a AED 150,000 cheque bounced for insufficient funds. After a 01/09/2026 notarial demand received 03/09/2026 with no payment, claimant filed on 06/10/2026 seeking principal plus 6% yearly late interest, fees and costs. Hearing set 24/11/2026.

## Key facts

- Claimant agreed with defendant under 10/01/2026 supply agreement to supply building materials (rebar and cement) to Al Khawanij project (page 1)
- Claimant issued three invoices totalling AED 506,500 received by defendant without reservation (page 1)
- Defendant paid AED 120,000 by bank transfer on 10/05/2026 (page 1)
- Cheque No. 004512 for AED 150,000 dated 30/06/2026 returned unpaid on 02/07/2026 for insufficient funds and does not count as payment (page 1)
- Remaining outstanding balance is AED 386,500 after deducting only bank payment (page 3, handwriting)
- Claimant formally notified defendant on 01/09/2026 via notary, received on 03/09/2026, with no payment made (page 1, handwriting)
- Parties agreed in clause 9 of agreement to 6% annual late interest, and claimant does not claim compound interest (page 2)
- Claimant seeks compensation for returned cheque under Article 712 plus fees, expenses and attorney fees (page 2)
