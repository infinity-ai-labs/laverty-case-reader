# Wamdat Al Noor Electrical Equipment Trading LLC vs Aamidat Al Shorouq Contracting LLC - claim for three returned cheques and validation of precautionary attachment

Wamdat Al Noor claims Aamidat Al Shorouq issued three cheques under a 10/02/2026 supply agreement on Al Kuthban National Bank totalling 297,750 AED which were dishonoured for insufficient funds and signature mismatch, evidenced by bank certificate dated 03/09/2026. Applicant obtained a 09/09/2026 precautionary attachment in application 412/2026 over respondent bank accounts up to that amount, conditional on filing a proof action within 8 days, then filed this commercial action before Sharjah Federal Court of First Instance on 14-15/09/2026 seeking validation of attachment, payment with 5% interest, and costs and fees.

## Key facts

- Under supply agreement dated 10/02/2026 defendant issued three cheques to claimant which were dishonoured (page 1)
- Cheques drawn on Al Kuthban National Bank: 000731 for 96,400 AED due 15/06/2026, 000732 for 112,750 AED due 15/07/2026, 000733 for 88,600 AED due 15/08/2026, total 297,750 AED (page 1)
- Return reasons: insufficient funds for first two cheques and signature mismatch for third, with dated return memo on each cheque (page 2)
- Presentation on 15/06/2026, 15/07/2026, 17/08/2026 and return on 16/06/2026, 16/07/2026, 18/08/2026 via electronic clearing (page 2)
- Bank notified Central Bank of account holder data for the two cheques returned for insufficient funds (page 2)
- On 09/09/2026 urgent judge in application 412/2026 ordered precautionary attachment of respondent balances with UAE banks up to 297,750 AED (page 3, handwriting)
- Commercial claim was filed on 15/09/2026 per filing stamp, within 8-day period from attachment order (page 1, stamp)
