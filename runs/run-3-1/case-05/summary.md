# Wamdat Al Noor v Aamdat Al Shurooq - Returned cheques claim AED 297,750

Wamdat Al Noor claims AED 297,750 from Aamdat Al Shurooq for three cheques on Al Kuthban National Bank returned for insufficient funds and signature mismatch, plus 5% interest, costs and fees, and asks to confirm the 09/09/2026 conservatory seizure in request 412/2026 which required a proof action within 8 days. Bank advice of 03/09/2026 proves return. Claim filed 15/09/2026.
