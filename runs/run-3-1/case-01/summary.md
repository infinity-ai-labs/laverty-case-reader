# Samer Khalil Al-Haddad v Najmat Al Khaleej General Contracting LLC

Partial labour case 1234/2026 before Dubai Labour Court of First Instance: Jordanian senior accountant employed since 2019 on AED 18,000 claims unpaid wages from June 2026 and termination on 15/08/2026 shortly after MOHRE complaint, without notice. Claims AED 196,460 for wages, notice, gratuity, leave and unlawful-termination compensation plus experience certificate and costs. On 27/10/2026 defendant counsel appeared and sought time; court adjourned to 17/11/2026 for reply, ordering reply by 10/11/2026 and production of contract and Jan-Aug 2026 payrolls.
