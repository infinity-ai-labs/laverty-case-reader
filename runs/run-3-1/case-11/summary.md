# Al Bareeq Al Faddi Kitchen Equipment Trading v Layali Al Marsa Restaurant Management – Payment Order Petition for AED 293,450

Al Bareeq Al Faddi seeks a payment order against Layali Al Marsa for AED 293,450 as the net price of commercial kitchen equipment after a returned-goods credit, plus 5% yearly interest from the 12/10/2026 petition and costs. It relies on three invoices, a written debt acknowledgment dated 06/08/2026 signed by the respondent's General Manager promising payment by 31/08/2026, and a notarial demand dated 01/10/2026 with a 7-day grace period that expired unpaid, under Articles 143 and 144 of Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022.

## Key facts

- The claimant supplied the respondent with commercial kitchen equipment for its Sharjah branches, which was received without reservation under three invoices. (page 1)
- Invoices: SG-2026-041 for AED 126,000 dated 09/03/2026, SG-2026-077 for AED 104,250 dated 21/04/2026, and SG-2026-112 for AED 88,200 dated 02/06/2026, less credit note CN-2026-019 for AED (25,000) dated 18/06/2026, leaving a net of AED 293,450. (page 1)
- The respondent acknowledged the debt in writing dated 06/08/2026 signed by its General Manager Mr. Wael Jamal Al-Khatib, confirming receipt of the equipment complete and in good condition and that the debt is established, due and undisputed. (page 2)
- The respondent undertook to pay the debt in one payment no later than 31/08/2026. (page 2)
- The claimant demanded payment by a notice served via the Notary Public on 01/10/2026 granting seven days to pay, which expired without payment. (page 1)
