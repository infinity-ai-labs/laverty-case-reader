## Key facts

- First defendant ran the red light at Umm Suqeim/Al Wasl intersection in Dubai and hit the left side of claimant's vehicle which was crossing on green. (page 2)
- Traffic report assigned 100% liability to first vehicle driver (first defendant) and found second driver (claimant) not at fault. (page 2)
- Claimant suffered right leg fractures and right ankle fracture with comminuted tibia/fibula fractures plus chest/left arm bruises, hospitalised 16/06/2026-29/06/2026, underwent internal fixation on 17/06/2026 then physiotherapy. (page 3)
- Claimant left with 15% permanent partial disability of right lower limb with right ankle stiffness, limited movement and slight limp. (page 3, handwriting)
- Traffic misdemeanor court convicted first defendant on 28/07/2026 in case 3471/2026 and fined him 10,000 AED; judgment became final per 03/09/2026 certificate. (page 1)
- At-fault vehicle insured with second defendant Sanam Al-Rimal Insurance PSC under policy No. 000000. (page 1)
- Claimant had sick leave 16/06/2026-15/10/2026 and was deemed fit to return from 16/10/2026; claims 4 months salary x 19,500 AED. (page 3)
