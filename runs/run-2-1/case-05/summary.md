# Wamdat Al Noor Electrical Equipment Supply LLC v Aamdat Al Shurooq Contracting LLC – Commercial claim for AED 297,750 in returned cheques and confirmation of precautionary attachment

Wamdat Al Noor claims AED 297,750 for three cheques issued by Aamdat Al Shurooq under a 10/02/2026 supply deal and dishonoured (two for insufficient funds, one for signature mismatch) per bank certificate dated 03/09/2026, plus 5% interest, costs and fees, and seeks confirmation of the precautionary attachment granted 09/09/2026 in Request 412/2026 which required filing the merits action within 8 days; the claim was registered 15/09/2026.

## Key facts

- Under supply agreement dated 10/02/2026 defendant issued three cheques to claimant returned unpaid totalling AED 297,750. (page 1)
- Cheque 000731 dated 15/06/2026 for AED 96,400 returned for insufficient funds at Al Kuthban National Bank. (page 1)
- Cheque 000732 dated 15/07/2026 for AED 112,750 returned for insufficient funds. (page 1)
- Cheque 000733 dated 15/08/2026 for AED 88,600 returned for signature mismatch. (page 1)
- Bank certified cheques were presented and returned unpaid with return statement affixed, recording presentation 15/06/2026, 15/07/2026, 17/08/2026 and return 16/06/2026, 16/07/2026, 18/08/2026. (page 2)
- On 09/09/2026 urgent judge ordered precautionary attachment on defendant balances in Request No. 412/2026. (page 1)
- Urgent judge decided to impose precautionary attachment on respondent balances up to AED 297,750 and required applicant to file proof-of-right action within 8 days. (page 3, handwriting)
