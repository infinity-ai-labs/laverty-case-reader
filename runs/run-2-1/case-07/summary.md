# Mazen Ibrahim Al-Attar v Hisham Tawfiq Al-Badri and Sanam Al-Rimal Insurance - Traffic Accident Compensation

Civil compensation claim for a 16/06/2026 Dubai traffic accident. Claimant Mazen Al-Attar says first defendant ran a red light and hit his car, causing fractures and 15% permanent disability. First defendant was finally convicted in traffic court. Claimant seeks AED 346,640 jointly from driver and insurer (medical, lost wages, disability, moral damages) under the new Civil Transactions Law and Evidence Law.

## Key facts

- On 16/06/2026 around 21:40 at Umm Suqeim-Al Wasl intersection in Dubai, first vehicle ran red light and hit left side of claimant's vehicle which was crossing on green. (page 1)
- Traffic report assigned 100% liability to first vehicle driver (first defendant) and found second driver (claimant) not at fault. (page 2)
- Claimant suffered comminuted tibia/fibula fracture in right leg and right ankle fracture with bruises, had internal fixation surgery on 17/06/2026 and stayed in hospital until 29/06/2026. (page 3)
- Claimant left with 15% permanent partial disability in right lower limb with partial ankle stiffness, limited movement and slight limp. (page 3)
- Traffic Misdemeanor Court convicted first defendant on 28/07/2026 in case 3471/2026 and fined him AED 10,000; judgment became final per 03/09/2026 certificate. (page 1)
- First vehicle insured with second defendant (Sanam Al-Rimal Insurance) under policy No. 000000. (page 1)
- Claimant had sick leave from 16/06/2026 to 15/10/2026, had 36 physio sessions from 06/07/2026 to 30/09/2026, and was fit to return to work from 16/10/2026. (page 3)
