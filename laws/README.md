The 1985 Civil Transactions Law carries a `status` note: it was repealed and replaced from 1 June 2026, but it still governs contracts concluded before that date. A citation of it is flagged so a lawyer checks which law governs the facts.

For each law it holds the full list of article numbers, which the citation check uses to tell whether a cited article exists, and the official Arabic and English text of the articles the test files cite, which it shows next to the citation and compares with any quoted wording.

Arabic is the authoritative text; the English is the government's official courtesy translation. The texts come from LAVERTY's law library, which holds them as published on the UAE Legislation platform (uaelegislation.gov.ae) and the Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae). The link to each law's official page is in its `source` field.

A citation to a law that is not in this index is labelled "not checked"; it is never passed as valid.
